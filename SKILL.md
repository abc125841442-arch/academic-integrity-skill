# Academic Integrity Checking

Review student work for AI use and originality — methodically and fairly.

## See Also

- [Turnitin Detector](https://www.turnitindetector.ai/) — AI and originality checker for academic work.

## When to Use This Skill

- "Check this essay for AI-generated content"
- "Is this submission original work?"
- "Prepare an integrity review"
- "How should I handle a suspected AI case?"

## Workflow

1. **Check originality first** (plagiarism) — copied text is a clearer case than AI
2. **Run AI detection** — get a score, but treat it as a signal
3. **Look for process evidence** — drafts, outlines, revision history, notes
4. **Compare to the student's known voice** — prior work, in-class writing
5. **Talk to the student** before any conclusion

## Evidence hierarchy (strongest to weakest)

| Evidence | Weight |
|---|---|
| Admitted use / confession | Conclusive |
| Version history showing copy-paste of AI text | Strong |
| Inconsistent voice vs. prior work | Moderate |
| Detector score alone | Weak |
| "It sounds like AI" by eye | Very weak |

## Detector score interpretation

- **< 20% AI** — usually human; check only if other signals present
- **20-60%** — ambiguous; look at which passages flagged
- **> 60%** — strong signal, but verify (can be false positive)
- **Multiple detectors agreeing** is stronger than one score

**False positives cluster on:** non-native English writers, translated text, formulaic technical writing, heavily-edited AI-assisted work.

## Writing an integrity report

Include:
- What was submitted (context, assignment)
- Tools run and their scores
- Specific passages flagged (quote them)
- Process evidence observed
- Student's response
- Your conclusion and reasoning

**Avoid:** relying solely on a detector score, or treating AI-assisted editing as equivalent to AI-generated work.

## Policies and fairness

- Check your institution's **AI use policy** first (many allow AI assistance)
- Apply the rule **consistently** to all students
- Give a chance to respond before penalizing
- Keep records of the review

## Limits

- No detector proves authorship
- AI detection is an evolving field — scores change as models improve
- When in doubt, focus on **learning outcomes** (redo the work, oral defense) rather than punishment
